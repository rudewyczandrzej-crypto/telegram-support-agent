# AI Support Agent Demo Knowledge Base

## Company
Company name: DemoShop. DemoShop is an example online store used for testing an AI support agent.

## Working hours
Support works Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:00.

## Delivery in Poland
Delivery in Poland costs 15 PLN. Delivery usually takes 1–3 business days.

## International delivery
International delivery is available only inside the European Union. International delivery usually takes 4–8 business days. The final cost depends on the country.

## Returns
Customers can return products within 14 days after receiving the order. The product must be unused and in original packaging.

## Refunds
Refunds are processed within 3–7 business days after the returned product is received and checked.

## Warranty
Products have a 24-month warranty unless the product page says otherwise.

## Payment methods
Available payment methods: card payment, BLIK, bank transfer, PayPal.

## Order tracking
Customers receive a tracking link by email after the order is shipped.

## Damaged package
If a package arrives damaged, the customer should take photos of the package and contact support with the order number.

## Escalation rules
If the customer asks about legal issues, individual discounts, complaints, chargebacks, personal data deletion, or anything not covered in this knowledge base, create a ticket for a human support agent.
